The location

At Brunneneck, in the heart of Berlin.

A central neighbourhood with many influences – lively and relaxed at once. Brunneneck lies in the middle of the Rosenthaler Vorstadt, combining the energy of the city centre with the calm of neighbourhood life.

Directly connected to the S-Bahn and U-Bahn network, close to parks, restaurants and the cultural life of the capital.
