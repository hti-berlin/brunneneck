On the 4th floor of the Brunneneck, a 4.5-room apartment of approx. 118 m² is ready for its first occupants after a high-quality renovation. Herringbone parquet, original panelled doors, arched windows and a calm colour palette bring together turn-of-the-century character and modern comfort.

Location

A quiet side street, right by the U-Bahn.

The Brunneneck stands on the corner of Brunnenstraße and Rheinsberger Straße – on a quiet side street in the heart of the neighbourhood, between Mauerpark, Arkonaplatz and Rosenthaler Platz.

Cafés, restaurants, galleries, schools and weekly markets are all within walking distance. The U8 station Bernauer Straße is directly opposite.
